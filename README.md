# Phase — Your chain. Your coin. Your rules.

Phase lets anyone launch their own sovereign blockchain from an Android phone. Name your asset, set your supply, hit mint — your own chain is provisioned in about a minute. List in the marketplace, accept swap offers, and track your Soft Net Worth from the dashboard.

## How it works

1. **Go Live** — name your asset, set your supply, mint. A sovereign chain is provisioned per issuer, isolated from everyone else's.
2. **Mint** — 1,000,000 shares minted at genesis on your chain. Your chain, your rules.
3. **Trade** — list in the marketplace and accept swap offers peer-to-peer.

## The fee, stated plainly

Flat **0.80%** on eligible transactions — shown before you confirm, nowhere else. The buyer pays the fee (receives coins minus the fee); the seller keeps 100% of proceeds. No hidden spreads.
