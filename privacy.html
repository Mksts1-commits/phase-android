Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026.

What Phase is

Phase lets users issue their own coins, browse a marketplace of user-issued coins, keep a watchlist, and manage swap offers, from the Phase web app or through an AI agent. Phase operates in test mode: no real money moves through Phase rails, and nothing on Phase is a real-money balance, price, or return.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, and legal name when you sign an issuer agreement.
  • Coin and profile content: coin names, tickers, taglines, theses, website and social links, logos, and documents you upload as proof (for example licenses, ownership documents, or reports).
  • Wallet information: blockchain wallet addresses and deposit addresses issued for testnet funding (Circle testnet wallets).
  • Usage and ledger records: marketplace activity, watchlist entries, offers, trades, and Phase Cred inputs — all computed from real records on file, never from claims alone.
  • Technical information: session tokens, local preferences (such as the light/dark theme choice), and ordinary server logs.

What we do not collect

We do not collect or store your bank login credentials. Bank connectivity, if ever enabled, runs through its provider's own flow. We do not collect payment card details through Phase. Test-mode funding uses testnet stablecoins only. We never ask you for a password, API key, or secret by email, chat, or social message. Anyone who does is not Phase.

How we use information

To run your account, your coins, and the marketplace; to compute Phase Cred from records on file and show you what proof is missing; to send account emails, such as email-verification codes; and to operate agent access you authorize.

Agent (connector) access

When you connect Phase to an AI agent, the agent acts with an OAuth token scoped to what you approved (for example dashboard reads, watchlist changes, or issuance). You can revoke that token at any time from the agent's side or by contacting support; revocation stops further agent access. Agents never see your password.

Service providers

Phase runs on third-party infrastructure, including Render (hosting), Supabase (database), Circle (testnet wallets), SendGrid (account emails), and GitHub Pages (landing page). Each provider processes only the data needed for its function, under its own terms.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the providers above, to operate the service; with a marketplace audience, for content you choose to publish (coin listings, profiles, and public proof are public by design); and when required by law or to protect the service from abuse.

Your choices

You may ask for a copy of your account information or for your account to be closed by contacting support. Closing an account does not erase marketplace history: coins you issued and their public records may remain visible as marketplace history. You may decline any optional proof upload; declining may lower the coin's Phase Cred, which is informational only.

Children

Phase is not directed at children. Accounts are for users of the age of majority in their jurisdiction.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will post the new version with a new effective date. Continued use after a change means you accept it.
