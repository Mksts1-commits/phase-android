Support

How to reach us

X (primary public channel): @phaseplatforms

What to include in a report

  • The email address on your Phase account.
  • What happened, in your own words.
  • The steps that led to it, and any error message shown.
  • The coin ticker or marketplace link, if the report is about a coin.

What to expect

Phase is in test mode. Support is best-effort; we do not promise response times or service levels. Reports about abuse, impersonation, or broken links help the whole marketplace and are prioritized.

Safety — please read

Phase will never ask you for a password, API key, wallet secret, or verification code by email, chat, or social message. Never share those with anyone claiming to be Phase support. If you believe your account is compromised, contact us from the channel above and change your password immediately.
